### Prerequisites:

1. A PS4 with firmware 13.52 or below firmware (if you have to/want to swap in a larger drive make sure to check the file you are using to reinstall the firmware is for 13.52 or under. I recommend at least a 500gb HDD)

2. A working internet connection as you will need the PS4 web browser for this to work.

### Pre-Exploit Setup:

 1. Make sure before you do anything, Remove all PSN accounts from your system as Sony can ban accounts that are on a modified system.

 2. Turn off automatic updates to prevent your system from automatically updating to a incompatible firmware revision. Settings -> System -> Automatic Downloads, And uncheck both boxes.

 ### Exploit steps:

 1. Once you have all the previous steps done launch the web browser from the PS4 Home Screen.

 2. Press Options on your controller, go to your browser settings, and select Delete Cookies and Clear Website Data. This prevents any pre-existing data from interrupting the exploit.

 3. Type in either "https://raw13g.github.io/" or "webkitty.arabpixel.net", these are the sites that will handle sideloading the payload to the PS4 system.

 4. Once either website loads it will likely prompt you to press the "X" button on your controller to confirm you want to sideload the payload.

 5. If Successful you will see a gold banner with the toast "GoldHen Loaded"


 ### Troubleshooting:

 Q: "I got a notification saying "Not enough free system memory" what do I do?"
 A: Simply press "OK" and try again.

 Q: "My system has frozen and isn't responding what do i do?"
 A: As it's a web exploit it isnt 100% stable, Hold the physical power button until your system fully powers down, turn it back on and try it again from step 1 of the exploit steps.

 Q: "I rebooted my PS4 and the goldhen menu is gone and i cant launch my homebrew apps"
 A: It isnt a permanent softmod, i recommend bookmarking the website you chose so you can easily load the website and load GoldHen again.
